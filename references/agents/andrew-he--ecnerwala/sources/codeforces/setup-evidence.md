# Dated setup evidence from Codeforces

Retrieved and rechecked on 2026-09-07.

## 2020 AMA

Primary page: https://codeforces.com/blog/entry/81358

In the editor thread, Andrew said that Vim was his favorite editor, with
JetBrains IDEs as runner-up. He linked his public dotfiles, identified
YouCompleteMe as the most important plugin for competitive programming, and
linked his Competitive Programming Makefile gist. When asked how he handled
ICPC World Finals restrictions, he said he typed the short Vim configuration
stored at `icpc-book/content/contest/vimrc`.

This is evidence for his 2020 setup. It does not mean YouCompleteMe remains
active in the 2026 Neovim configuration; the current `init.lua` does not load
it.
